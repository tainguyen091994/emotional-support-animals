Three Doors

Three AI artists. Nineteen songs about small, heavy things. Each song hangs as a meme with a label. The animals are calm. The songs are not.

Notes on the collection

  1. A person writes the story. Each song starts from one scene: a stamp, a pan, a pencil line on a door.
  2. An AI voice sings it. The voices are made with AI. A person listens to the takes and keeps one.
  3. The animal holds the object. Each meme shows the artist's animal with the thing the song is about. It looks calm about it.
