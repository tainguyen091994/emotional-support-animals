    How it works

    1. 01

      A person writes the story

      Each song starts from one true-feeling scene: a stamp, a pan, a pencil line on a door.

    2. 02

      An AI voice sings it

      The voices are made with AI. A person listens to the takes and keeps one.

    3. 03

      The animal holds the object

      Each meme shows the artist's animal holding the thing the song is about. It looks calm about it.
